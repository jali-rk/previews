How the evening goes

From raw spices to a full table

1of 5
  1. Step 1

    Arrive at our home

    We send you the location on WhatsApp. Tuk-tuk drivers in Sigiriya can bring you to the door.

  2. Step 2

    Meet the kitchen

    Smell the spices, see the vegetables, and learn what goes into each curry.

  3. Step 3

    Watch or help

    Scrape coconut, stir the pots, temper the dhal. Join in as much or as little as you like.

  4. Step 4

    Ten-plus dishes

    Curries, mallums, sambols and rice, cooked fresh in front of you.

  5. Step 5

    Eat together

    Sit down to the whole spread with tea. Ask for seconds. Everyone does.

The food

A table that doesn't fit on one photo

The menu follows the season and the market, so every evening is a little different. A typical spread might include:

  • Dhal curry
  • Jackfruit curry
  • Pumpkin curry
  • Green apple or mango curry
  • Gotukola mallum
  • Pol sambol
  • Brinjal moju
  • Beetroot curry
  • Papadam
  • Red rice
  • Chicken or fish on request
