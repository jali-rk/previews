The menu

Grilled, curried, or both.

From our menu board: fresh fish, prawns, calamari and lobster, grilled or cooked the Sri Lankan way. What's on depends on what the sea gives us.

  • Prawns

    Grilled, battered, hot battered or grilled jumbo, with chips and salad or vegetable fried rice.

  • Lobster

    Grilled, thermidor, or Sri Lankan lobster curry with coconut sambol and steamed rice.

  • Fresh fish

    Grilled tuna, red snapper or mullet, with chips and salad or vegetable fried rice.

  • Calamari

    Grilled, battered or hot battered, the way you like it.

  • Starters & omelettes

    Prawn cocktail, garlic kankun, tuna salad, and seafood or Sri Lankan omelettes.

  • Sunset drinks

    Cold beer, fresh juices and cocktails on the sand.

Market price. Seafood is priced by weight and by what came in today. Ask your server, or message us before you come.
