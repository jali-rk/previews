About the salon

One bride at a time, like family.

Recognised at the Sri Lanka Wedding Awards 2024–2025

Wathsala Tennakoon Salon is a bridal and beauty salon in Kurunegala, known for traditional Kandyan dressing done properly: the osariya pleats, the seven-chain necklaces, the nalal patiya sitting just right.

We work in a calm, family-style atmosphere and take time to learn what you want. Classic or modern, soft or editorial, your look is planned with you and tested before the day.

Bridal looks

Find your style

Kandyan bride

The full traditional look in a deep maroon, white or gold osariya, finished with Kandyan jewellery and a jasmine-framed hairstyle.

  • Osariya draping and pleating
  • Full Kandyan jewellery dressing, including nalal patiya
  • Long-wear bridal makeup for photos and heat

Indian / saree bride

Rich colour, statement eyes and a beautifully pleated saree, styled for a bright, celebratory look.

  • Saree draping in Indian or Kandyan style
  • Bold eye makeup with a long-lasting base
  • Bun or braid styling with fresh flowers

Western bride

Soft, clean bridal makeup with a polished updo or loose waves, designed to work with your gown and veil.

  • Natural or editorial makeup
  • Veil and hair accessory setting
  • Touch-up kit guidance for the reception

Homecoming

A fresh second look for your homecoming or going-away, lighter but still camera-ready.

  • Saree or frock styling
  • Hair restyle with accessories
  • Makeup refresh to suit evening light
