    Our kitchen

    Not a restaurant. A family home.

    When you eat at Shirani's, you eat what the family eats: rice with a generous spread of vegetable curries, dhal and sambol, cooked fresh that day.

    Guests often tell us it's the best rice and curry of their whole trip around Sri Lanka. Everything is made to order, so please message us before you come.
