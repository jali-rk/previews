Welcome home

Like staying with family in the hills

Rustle Hill is run by a Sri Lankan family who love sharing Ella with their guests. Expect help with train tickets and tuk-tuks, tips on the best viewpoints, and a quiet night's sleep to the sound of the hills.

Mornings at Rustle Hill

The breakfast guests write home about

Served fresh by the family with a view of the hills. Sri Lankan or Western, just tell us the night before.

  • Sri Lankan breakfast: hoppers, string hoppers, dhal and coconut sambol
  • Western breakfast: eggs your way, toast and jam
  • Fresh tropical fruit and a pot of Ceylon tea
  • Early train or hike? Ask for a packed breakfast
